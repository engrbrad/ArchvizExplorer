# Glossary

*Updated through ch89.*

## The Veil / The Hero Ritual
Unchanged from original planning — see prior entries. Althea's ritual misfired and pulled Leandro instead of a true Hero; the crown's cover-up of that failure is the root cause the Demon Kin exploit to time their assault on Selvane (ch06, ch16).

## Rank System (Novice → Apprentice → Adept → Expert → Master)
Unchanged — control/efficiency matter as much as raw affinity. Guild adventurer ranks (E through at least D, C, B) run in parallel; Leandro starts E-rank, is later promoted by Voss for "value delivered beyond contract scope" rather than combat merit.

## Mana Corruption / The Demon Kin
Unchanged in principle. Refined by ch21: the Demon Kin's **Fourth Seat, Morvain**, has been deliberately *engineering* corrupted-beast migrations in Ravencourt's western territory as a calculated strategic distraction — meaning not all "mana corruption" incidents in the story are natural; some are manufactured warfare.

## Soul-Bound Items
New mechanic (ch10). Rare magic items that "choose" their wielder on contact — cannot be stolen or given away once bound. Two found in the Millbrook cave's final chamber:
- **Leandro's ring** — grants spatial/storage magic (functionally the story's "Storage Box," as originally planned). Normally this ability only manifests naturally in mages who've pushed their affinity to Expert/Master rank over decades; the ring is a shortcut. Confirmed "substantial" capacity — not infinite, but large enough to matter enormously for someone previously limited to what he could carry.
- **Cassian's sword** — chose him instantly. Amplifies whoever holds it (not just Cassian — dramatically demonstrated when thrown to Percival mid-fight in ch33, who described feeling his own magic "responding differently... stronger, more focused") and can reconfigure its physical form.
- A third item, a **bracelet**, was also recovered — amplifies an existing mage's power rather than granting a new one. Later used by Seraphina in the Threxis fight (ch60), boosting her fire output to its "genuine limit."

## The Cave's Warding Trap
The final chamber's relics sat inside an ownership-test ward: grabbing an item quickly/greedily triggers lethal judgment; approaching slowly and deliberately is tolerated. Killed Fessin and Corwin (ch10) instantly and without visible violence.

## Crystals / Monster Cores
Harvested from defeated monsters (first mentioned ch27, dungeon floor 16–20 clears), sold through guild exchanges at rates depending on size/purity. Became the base fuel source for Leandro's weapons program starting around ch51–55.

## Dragon Stones
A rarer, denser, more naturally stable alternative to ordinary refined crystals — first mentioned via a contact named **Ophelia** (ch57). The stated goal of the Selvane vault search (ch58–64): if ordinary crystals produce an uncontrolled, cascading "chain reaction" (see below), a purer core might make the same output *containable* rather than catastrophic. Historically, Althea's crown vault held exactly three, treated as ceremonial royal relics rather than a resource anyone understood how to use.

## The Device / "The Compression Principle"
Leandro's weapon program (ch51 onward, full reveal ch54–57, first deployed ch65). Built from:
- A precision-forged containment housing (Borgrim)
- Refined crystal cores as fuel (Mirelle)
- A synchronization/trigger mechanism Leandro designed and, as of ch89, keeps deliberately secret even from his own team

**Mechanism (explicitly framed in nuclear terms, ch57):** multiple crystal "chambers" discharge in careful synchronization, and the effect isn't simple additive energy release — it's a "cascading resonance" where each core's discharge accelerates the others', described on-page as "not fission. But functionally, conceptually — the same runaway principle." A full-scale test with six refined-but-not-dragon-stone cores erased an entire island. The "single-person portable" version used to kill the Demon King in ch65 is described as smaller/more field-deployable, but still fortress-leveling.

As of ch89, Leandro is having Borgrim build **twelve smaller "housing units"** under the false cover story of "mining/demolition contracts" — the true purpose (targeted, warned strikes against a noble war-profiteering conspiracy) is being withheld from Borgrim and, functionally, from Mirelle as well, though she's tacitly aware of the cover story's real function.

## The Court of Twelve
The Demon Kin's ruling war council — twelve numbered "Seats" around a black-stone hall. See bible/characters.md for the seated generals identified so far (Second, Fourth, Sixth, Eighth, Ninth [empty], Twelfth). Presided over by General Vharek Dromm, who is not confirmed to hold a numbered Seat himself. The Demon King sits above/outside this council entirely and is never shown in the chapters read except via the aftermath of his death.

## The Noble Conspiracy / Shell Network
Introduced ch87–88: a cross-kingdom network of noble houses skimming war-supply grain shipments through a shell holding company registered in Thessary, using deliberately obscured, multi-layered ownership. Doran had started investigating irregular financial patterns near an eastern garrison town before his party was murdered to bury the discovery. Traced by Mirelle and Seraphina's contacts to a planned gathering/summit of the network's "major players," roughly six weeks out as of ch88 — the target of Leandro's current (ch89) production run.

## "The Last Watch" / "The Guildmaster's Stand"
The in-world name(s), already circulating on broadsheets within days, for Voss and the scattered guild adventurers who held Selvane's evacuation routes during the Fall of Selvane (ch16).
