# Glossary

## The Veil
The barrier/boundary between Earth and this world. Characters refer to summoned outsiders as being pulled from "beyond the Veil."

## The Hero Ritual
A desperation-measure summoning ritual, performed once a generation by a kingdom under existential pressure — in this case Althea, due to its border with Demon Kin territory. Not ceremonial: it's meant to pull a "soul of great potential" who is prophesied to be "unbreakable and unburdened by our world's chains," capable of ending a war outright (the last true Hero closed a war that had already cost three kingdoms half their people).

Requires years of preparation (Althea's attempt took seven years) and precise conditions (alignment of the twin moons, a blood-sealed circle). **Cannot be reversed once it binds someone to the world** — confirmed by Rasmin in ch01: "not for another lifetime, at minimum."

Althea's ritual misfired: instead of pulling the intended Hero-caliber soul, it grabbed the "nearest available soul" (Leandro) due to some unspecified calibration failure. The crown chose to **cover up the failure** rather than admit it — most of Althea's own nobility still believe a Hero would answer if the true crisis came. (See Notes for the planned payoff: the Demon Kin learn of this cover-up via informants and use it as the linchpin of their invasion timing.)

## Rank System (locked mechanic — NOT a level-up/XP system)
Modeled after a "rune-bound professor"-style classification, chosen deliberately so it can be expanded/refined later. All magic users are classified along a scale:

**Novice → Apprentice → Adept → Expert → Master**

Key rule: raw affinity/power is only part of the ranking. Control and efficiency matter just as much — a disciplined Novice with clean technique can outperform a sloppy Apprentice who wastes half their strength. It's framed as "a floor you can build up from properly," not a hard ceiling.

Leandro's placement: **low Novice**, "possibly the lowest [Yolanda has] measured in years" in raw affinity, but with unusually clean control for one month of practice.

Guild adventurer ranks run in parallel/separately: **E-rank** is the standard bottom rank for new registrants (Leandro's starting rank); higher ranks (D, C, etc.) require party support and guild-vetted experience.

## Mana Corruption
The cause of monsters in this world — beasts twisted by mana corruption, found in wild places (mountains, deep forests, old ruins). Most are manageable by trained soldiers or adventurers; some are far more dangerous. Distinct from and lesser than the Demon Kin threat.

## The Demon Kin
Not folklore — an organized, hostile force that has pushed at the edges of human territory for longer than living memory accounts for. Ruled via a war council (see General Vharek Dromm, Characters) that plans in terms of years, using proxy bandit uprisings to strategically weaken border kingdoms like Althea before a direct strike. Territory lies north of Althea, past the mountain range known as the Fangs.

## Three-Horn Bear
A mana-corrupted monster: a bear-shaped creature far larger than its mundane counterpart, with three curved horns crowning a battering-ram skull, fast despite its size, and resistant enough that it took multiple solid sword strikes from a D-rank adventurer to bring down. First (and so far only) monster to appear on-page — nearly killed Leandro in ch04, triggering his first conscious discovery of the Phase ability.

## Storage Box
One of the four skills locked for Leandro (see Characters). Not yet unlocked in the current draft — planned to be gained via an ancient cave/ruins exploration arc (seeded in ch07, the Millbrook cave). Implies a prior otherworlder or ancient civilization once had access to abilities like his.
