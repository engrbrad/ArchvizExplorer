# Characters

*Updated through ch89 ("The Plan He Chose Instead"), the current end of the draft.*

## Leandro Valdez
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Former life:** nuclear engineer on Earth. Isekai'd mid-errand by Althea's misfiring Hero-summoning ritual (original draft name "Leo Cortez," retired ch01).
- **Personality (still core):** methodical, checklist-driven, calm under pressure, assesses danger like a hazard checklist, values honesty and firsthand data over secondhand assurance.
- **Original ethical line (ch04):** refused to introduce any tech/weapon that could "tip the balance" of the world's power structures.
- **What actually happened to that line (major arc development):** he broke it. After witnessing Guildmaster Voss's death holding the line at Selvane (ch16), and unable to shake the guilt of the ritual's failure, he began — starting around ch51–57 — designing and building a crystal-powered explosive device with Borgrim and Mirelle. A full-scale test erased an entire uninhabited island (ch57), confirming the device's output is "an order of magnitude beyond" projections — explicitly framed in-text as a chain-reaction, nuclear-weapon analog. He used a refined version to solo-infiltrate a Demon Kin fortress by glider and kill, without knowing it in advance, the Demon King himself (ch65). As of ch89, grief over Doran's party's murder pushed him to the edge of a plan to indiscriminately wipe out an entire noble conspiracy in one strike — he pulled back before committing to it, but is now knowingly deceiving Borgrim and Mirelle about the true scope of a 12-unit production run of smaller devices, intended for "precision," warned strikes against the people responsible. **This is the story's live central moral thread as of the current chapter — track its resolution carefully.**
- **Phase:** innate, involuntary/reflexive, headache cost (worse with repeated use). First discovered ch04 (three-horn bear). Recurs at other moments of mortal danger (e.g., the warded cave dais, ch10).
- **Water Manipulation:** learned gradually from ch02 (Yolanda), low-Novice affinity but clean control. Used practically throughout (antidote administration, clearing silt, emergency medicine) more than combat.
- **Earth Shaping:** locked in original planning (ch-brainstorming) as Leandro's slow, construction-only, fatigue-costed ability — **not clearly shown on-page for Leandro specifically yet**. Note: ch33 attributes "earth affinity" combat use (a hasty stone wall) to **Cassian**, not Leandro — flag as a continuity question (see plot/notes.md).
- **Storage Box → became a soul-bound storage ring** (ch10): found in the Millbrook ancient cave, chooses its wearer, grants spatial/storage magic that normally takes mages a lifetime to earn naturally. Confirmed "substantial" capacity. This satisfies the original "Storage Box, unlocked via ancient cave" plan, just reskinned as an item rather than a raw ability.
- **Guilt thread (as planned back in early outlining):** fully paid off starting ch16 — the Fall of Selvane, Voss's death, and the unanswerable "would a real Hero have changed this" question become his explicit, ongoing emotional throughline, and are the stated root of his eventual decision to build the device.
- **Current arc position (ch89):** grieving Doran's party's murder, walking back from an indiscriminate-strike plan, now running a partially deceptive weapons production effort with his own trusted team. Unresolved.

## Cassian
- **Role:** buddy / secondary protagonist
- **Established personality (unchanged):** shameless, sharp-eyed, self-appointed guide, needles Leandro but respects his lines once explained.
- **Ability (was "TBD" — now developed on-page):** a soul-bound sword recovered from the same Millbrook cave chamber that gave Leandro his ring (ch10) — chose him instantly, no hesitation. The sword amplifies whoever wields it (shown dramatically in ch33, growing and channeling Percival's wind magic when thrown to him mid-fight) and can reconfigure its own form. Also has an **earth affinity** of his own, used defensively in combat (ch33) — see continuity flag above about whether this overlaps with Leandro's planned Earth Shaping.
- **Hidden backstory (major reveal, ch85 "Aldervine"):** strongly implied — not yet stated outright — to be the disappeared son of **House Ashford**, a minor noble house that once held the Aldervine region until a grain-hoarding scandal destroyed the family's standing. Left before the worst of the fallout, chose an ordinary/wandering life over defending or redeeming a disgraced name. Leandro has pieced this together but has not told Cassian he knows.
- **Relationship to Leandro:** deepened significantly — present for the cave deaths (ch10), the Selvane grief (ch16), building the device, the Island test, and is now the person actively trying to pull Leandro back from the edge after Doran's party's murder (ch88).

Flag as an open thread — the story's own early planning (pre-ch16) intended a deliberate reveal of Rasmin/Yolanda's fates for emotional weight; check whether later chapters (57–89, not fully read) address this.

## Yolanda

## Guildmaster Aldric Voss
- **Role:** Selvane guild hall's guildmaster, introduced ch11.
- **Personality:** weathered, patient, reads people rather than reports — promoted Leandro for "unusually high value delivered beyond contract scope" rather than combat merit.
- **Fate:** dies in ch16, "The Fall of Selvane" — holds the palace district's main evacuation gate personally, single-handedly kills a seated member of the Demon Kin's Court of Twelve (the **Ninth Seat**, unnamed), then dies fighting a second general (**Kaelthorn, the Twelfth Seat**) after already being critically wounded, buying time for thousands of civilians to escape. Remembered as "The Last Watch" / "The Guildmaster's Stand." **This death is the direct catalyst for Leandro's guilt and, eventually, his weapons program.**

## Petra, Dagen, Tolben (Millbrook)
- **Petra** — village elder, first met ch07. Millbrook's "bigger problem" (the unfinished cutoff line from the earlier draft) turned out to be mundane and severe: labor shortage from monster attacks and northern-watch conscription, compounding a failing well and untended fields — resolved through Leandro's engineering work (ch08).
- **Dagen** — Millbrook's blacksmith, works with Leandro to rebuild the well's pulley system.
- **Tolben** — a Millbrook farmer, explains the labor shortage behind the failing fields.

## Fessin and Corwin
- Two guild-assigned cave surveyors who accompany Leandro and Cassian into the Millbrook ruins (ch09). Both die in ch10 — an ancient warding trap judges their greedy, hurried attempt to grab relics from the final chamber's pedestals and kills them instantly. Leandro and Cassian carry their bodies out and report the deaths honestly to Voss, omitting only the ring's true nature.

## Yara
- Ostwick's eastern lock-gate keeper, 11 years on the post. Leandro fixes her mechanism's decade-old timing mismatch (ch14). Sees him and Cassian off warmly when they leave for Brindlemark (ch23).

## Doran's Party — Doran, Ilyana, Percival, Bram
- Established, respected Brindlemark dungeon-delving party that Leandro and Cassian join for a long expedition (floors 16 through 51+, ch24–42).
  - **Doran** — party leader, light-magic swordsman.
  - **Ilyana** — precise melee fighter.
  - **Percival** — wind-affinity mage; briefly wields Cassian's soul-bound sword mid-fight in ch33 with dramatic effect.
  - **Bram** — shield/tank; loses his arm below the elbow fighting the Demon Kin general **Threxis** at Selvane's ruins (ch60) — survives, is later confirmed **healed and reunited with his party** (per ch87).
  - **Ferrin** — the party's alchemist, source of the antidotes used against the floor-46 spider nest (ch33) — minor/background role.
- Party parts ways with Leandro and Cassian amicably after floor 51 (ch42), independently deciding to head toward the war front themselves rather than keep delving for its own sake.
- Reunites with Leandro's group for the Selvane ruins expedition (ch60) to search for dragon stones, fighting and killing Threxis (Eighth Seat) alongside Seraphina.
- **Fate: all five confirmed murdered** (ch87, "Silence from the North") — found dead near Corren's Watch, staged to look like a Demon Kin ambush, but with details (no tracks, valuables untouched, wounds inconsistent with Demon Kin, bodies gathered rather than scattered) that read as an execution. Traced (ch88) to a cross-kingdom noble/shell-company conspiracy skimming war-supply grain shipments — Doran had been investigating it. **This is the event that breaks Leandro's restraint and drives the current (ch89) moral crisis.**

## Borgrim Ironhelm
- **Role:** master dwarven smith, recruited via a letter from Doran (ch54) — "best precision work" his party has seen outside a royal armory, discreet, values sound thinking over flash.
- **Role in the weapon program:** builds the containment housings for Leandro's device from ch54 onward. Present for the Island test (ch57) — visibly shaken by its result ("We just erased an island... with something built in a workshop, by five people, in a few months").
- **As of ch89:** being deliberately misled by Leandro about the true purpose/scale of a new 12-unit production run (told it's for "mining/demolition contracts"). Leandro deliberately withholds the trigger/synchronization mechanism from him as a partial safeguard — and a sign he no longer fully trusts even himself.

## Mirelle
- **Role:** engineer/scientist, works alongside Borgrim on containment and crystal refinement — likely met through Ravencourt's academy (not yet confirmed on-page exactly how they met).
- **Key trait:** rigorous, clinical, the voice of caution in the workshop team. Corrected Leandro's early conduit geometry. Diagnosed the Island test's runaway cascade as a compounding "resonance" effect, not simple energy addition.
- **Critical moment (ch88):** her frightened "Leandro, please—" is what actually stops him mid-spiral from committing to the indiscriminate mass-strike plan against the noble conspiracy — arguably the most important intervention in the whole late arc.
- **As of ch89:** also being told the "demolition contract" cover story for the new production run, though the text notes she "already spoken with Borgrim and understood exactly what cover story she was meant to maintain," with private doubt she doesn't voice.

## Seraphina — "The Ember Blade"
- **Role:** the fire mage, near-Master rank, previously only known by reputation/nickname ("Ember Blade") in ch14's tavern rumors.
- Holds a defensive mountain pass near Ravencourt (ch45) — the Demon Kin's **Fourth Seat, Morvain**, has been *deliberately* feeding her engineered monster "migrations" for months as a strategic distraction to keep her and Ravencourt's attention off the real campaign (revealed from the Demon Kin POV in ch21) — she does not know this yet as of the chapters read.
- Joins Leandro's group properly by ch58–60: fights alongside them (with an amplifying bracelet from the Millbrook cave haul) to kill Threxis, the Eighth Seat, in Selvane's ruins.
- Provides crucial academy contacts/intelligence for tracing both dragon stones (ch58) and the noble conspiracy behind Doran's party's murder (ch88).
- Reacts to Doran's party's death with raw grief that breaks her usual composure (ch87) — genuinely loved them as found family, same as Leandro and Cassian.

## Ophelia
- Mentioned (ch57) as the source of Leandro's knowledge that "dragon stones" exist as a denser, more stable alternative fuel source. Not yet given an on-page introduction scene in the chapters read — likely an academy contact. Confirm/expand when read in full.

## Petrus

## Demon Kin — The Court of Twelve

A ruling council of twelve seated generals, each holding a numbered "Seat," presided over (though not clearly the First Seat himself) by **General Vharek Dromm**. Established across ch06, ch20–22, and referenced through ch60 and ch65–66.

- **Vharek Dromm** — patient, calculating strategist introduced ch06; conceived the plan to strike Selvane directly by exploiting the crown's cover-up of the failed Hero ritual. Presides over Court meetings, tracks his fellow generals' hidden risks and rivalries closely. Privately uneasy (ch22) about how varied and hard-to-control his own Court's individual agendas are becoming.
- **Serai** — Vharek's second/aide, sharp-tongued, more impatient than him. Not clearly a numbered Seat herself — functions as his intelligence conduit.
- **Kaelthorn — Twelfth Seat.** Young, arrogant, dismissive of human capability. Wounded badly by Voss at Selvane (ch16) while "finishing" him — downplays the wound's severity to the rest of the Court (ch20), though Vharek privately notes he's covering for how close it actually was.
- **The Ninth Seat** — unnamed. Killed outright by Voss during the Fall of Selvane (ch16/20). Seat left empty as of ch22; replacement not yet named.
- **Morvain — Fourth Seat**, "the Wolf of the Western Reach." Extremely controlled and patient; running the long-game distraction campaign keeping Seraphina and Ravencourt's western border chasing engineered monster incidents instead of the real campaign (ch21). Notes with something like respect that Seraphina is skilled enough to eventually see through the pattern — gives himself "two, perhaps three months" before she does.
- **Threll — Sixth Seat.** Spymaster; has planted long-term human-side assets (servants, merchants, minor officials, guild clerks) across kingdoms for years. Flags to the Court (ch21) that Ravencourt's academy is starting to ask "the right kind of questions" about the manufactured monster pattern.
- **Threxis — Eighth Seat.** Encountered and killed by Leandro's group (Doran's party + Seraphina) in Selvane's ruins while they search for dragon stones (ch60) — a very hard fight; costs Bram his arm.
- **Malgorath — Second Seat.** Enormous, ancient, essentially unconcerned with the entire war — sits on the Court because "Vharek asks politely," not out of investment in the outcome. Radiates a level of raw presence that silences the whole chamber on arrival. Framed explicitly as a sleeping threat far beyond anything else established in the story — "a blessing and a threat, wearing the same face," per Vharek's own private assessment. **Likely the story's biggest unfired Chekhov's gun.**
- **Third, Seventh, Eleventh Seats** — mentioned in the ch21 Court roll-call but not yet named or detailed (vague operations: corrupting a supply route, forces held in unstated reserve, unspecified "personal research").
- **The Demon King** — never named on-page in the chapters read. Killed by Leandro's solo glider-infiltration bombing raid on a fortress (ch65) — Leandro didn't know his target's true identity until the fact surfaced weeks later through Seraphina's contacts. The seated Court's reaction to this (ch66b, "The Empty Throne") shows Vharek among the first to arrive at the ruined stronghold, already grasping the scale of what's changed.
