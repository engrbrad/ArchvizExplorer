# Characters

## Leandro Valdez
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Former life:** nuclear engineer on Earth (night-shift control room work at a power plant) — methodical, checklist-driven, calm under pressure, more likely to be annoyed than scared by an anomaly
- **Isekai'd:** yanked mid-errand (cereal aisle, comparing oatmeal prices) by a botched Demon-Kin-adjacent... no — by Althea's Hero-summoning ritual, which misfired and pulled the "nearest available soul" instead of the intended Hero. Original name in the very first draft was "Leo Cortez" — retired in favor of **Leandro Valdez**, locked as of ch01.
- **Wants (external goal):** understand this world firsthand (refuses secondhand information/a desk job) before deciding how to be useful in it
- **Needs (internal/emotional truth):** to matter/contribute the way he did back home — his sense of self is tied to being quietly, competently useful
- **Flaw / lie they believe (planned, not yet on-page):** guilt that a "real" Hero might have prevented the palace's fall — a question the story is not meant to answer definitively
- **Personality:** nerdy, analytical, dry sense of humor, assesses danger like a hazard checklist instead of panicking, values control/efficiency over raw power, journals/lists things to think clearly
- **Ethical line (self-imposed):** will NOT introduce advanced Earth tech/weapons (explosives, propulsion, etc.) that could "tip the balance" of the world's power structures — deliberately restricts himself to civilian, quality-of-life engineering (water, sanitation, mechanical advantage) using only locally available materials
- **On women's spaces / phase temptation:** his phase ability could trivially let him access places like women's bathrooms — he actively refuses to use it that way even when tempted or goaded; this restraint is a core character-likability trait, not a joke at his expense
- **Arc:** starts as an open, unafraid-to-help outsider in Althea → after the Demon Kin's invasion escalates and more places come under attack, realizes he must act, but is still combat-weak, so he moves into "shadow work" (quiet, uncredited fixes/aid) → guilt over the palace's fall (see Notes) becomes a slow-burn emotional thread
- **First appears:** ch01
- **Key scenes:** the summoning (ch01), water-magic assessment / low-Novice reveal (ch02), declining the crown's job offer (ch03), guild registration + three-horn bear near-death / first phase trigger (ch04), decision to hide the phase ability (ch05)

### Skill set (locked)
1. **Phase** — innate from the very start (not learned). Involuntary/reflexive: triggers automatically when he's about to die, not on command. Purely defensive — can't act or attack while phased. **Cost:** a severe headache on activation, worse with each repeated use in a short window (spikes into a "detonation" behind the eyes by the 3rd trigger in ch04, knocking him unconscious). Meant to improve/soften over time as he "gets used to" it, but is a genuine liability early on. He hides this ability from everyone except Cassian (who witnessed it) — considers it too dangerous to be known, since "a man who can't be killed" invites exploitation by kingdoms/nobles/generals looking for leverage or a weapon.
2. **Water Manipulation** — learned gradually starting ch02, taught by Yolanda. Low-Novice affinity, "possibly the lowest in years" per Yolanda, but unusually clean control for a 1-month beginner. Ties directly into his engineering instincts (pressure, flow, hydraulics) rather than combat.
3. **Earth Shaping** — learned gradually, mid-game (not yet on-page). Slow, requires calm/concentration (unusable in combat or danger — opposite of Phase, which only works under danger), has a fatigue/physical cost like real manual labor. Construction-only: shaping/moving already-loose earth or existing stone/soil, no combat use, no instant walls or spikes. Fits his "underground bases / hidden shadow-work infrastructure" niche.
4. **Storage Box** — unlocked later specifically through an ancient-ruins/cave exploration arc (seeded starting ch07, the Millbrook cave). Not yet unlocked as of the current draft.

## Cassian
- **Role:** supporting — Leandro's buddy/foil
- **Introduced:** ch03 (named by Rasmin), first appears on-page ch04
- **Base of operations:** a corner table in Selvane's capital tavern; calls his work "logistics," everyone else calls it "knowing a guy for everything"
- **Personality:** wiry, sharp-eyed, shameless, zero filter, treats every situation as an opportunity — genuinely doesn't understand why Leandro won't use his powers more "practically" (spying, etc.) and needles him about it, but respects the line once Leandro actually explains his reasoning (ch04 tech-restraint conversation)
- **Ability:** not yet defined — intended to be flashy/combat-capable, in contrast to Leandro's non-combat utility set, so he can be "the face" in a fight while Leandro fixes what's actually broken
- **Relationship to Leandro:** self-appointed guide to "not dying stupidly," attached himself to Leandro out of curiosity, ends up genuinely caring — carried Leandro's unconscious body out of the three-horn bear fight and finished the kill alone (ch04); keeps Leandro's phase ability secret once asked (ch05)
- **First appears:** ch03 (named), ch04 (on-page)
- **Key scenes:** the tavern meeting (ch04), the "I won't give this world modern weapons" conversation (ch04), the three-horn bear fight aftermath (ch04–05), agreeing to keep the phase secret (ch05)

## Elder Rasmin
- **Role:** supporting — shrine elder, ran the failed Hero summoning ritual
- **Description:** older, silver-bearded, leans on a staff; the first to speak after the ritual fails ("This is not the Hero")
- **Personality:** direct, doesn't posture or deflect, revises his own assumptions readily once shown evidence (repeatedly described as "recalibrating" his read on Leandro)
- **Role in plot:** the one who tells Leandro the ritual can't be reversed; oversees his first month of integration/training; connects him with Yolanda and later Cassian; delivers supplies, coin, and a letter of introduction when Leandro chooses to travel instead of taking the crown's job offer
- **Fate (planned, not yet on-page):** presumed to be at the Selvane palace/shrine when the Demon Kin eventually attack — ties into Leandro's future guilt thread
- **First appears:** ch01

## Yolanda
- **Role:** supporting — mage instructor
- **Description:** brisk, no-nonsense woman in her forties, used to teaching children, unimpressed by adult methodical questioning (at first)
- **Role in plot:** tests and trains Leandro's water magic starting ch02; explains the rank system (Novice / Apprentice / Adept / Expert / Master — control and efficiency matter as much as raw affinity, so a disciplined Novice can outperform a sloppy Apprentice); assesses him as an extremely low-Novice affinity but unusually clean control
- **First appears:** ch02

## General Vharek Dromm
- **Role:** antagonist — Demon Kin general
- **Description:** not physically large as Demon Kin go; power comes from cold, patient calculation; survived three failed human incursions and a civil purge
- **Plan:** exploit the fact that Althea's court has covered up the Hero ritual's failure — most of their own nobility still believe a Hero would answer if the real crisis came, so their defense posture is built on a lie. Intends to strike Althea's capital directly (not a border skirmish) once conditions are right — estimated timeline "months, perhaps a year" from ch06.
- **First appears:** ch06

## Serai
- **Role:** supporting antagonist — Vharek's second-in-command
- **Personality:** younger, sharper-tongued, more impatient than Vharek; wants to strike immediately rather than wait
- **First appears:** ch06

## Petra

- **Description:** stooped, gray-haired, flatly exhausted — has clearly explained Millbrook's troubles to outsiders too many times already
- **Role in plot:** hosts Leandro/Cassian's cave-survey commission party; about to explain that Millbrook has "bigger problems" than the newly discovered ancient cave when the current draft cuts off
- **First appears:** ch07
