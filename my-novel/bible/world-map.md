# World Map

Setting: a classic sword-and-magic fantasy world — NOT a "regressed/lost technology" setting. This world simply never developed engineering/science thinking (pressure, leverage, sanitation, mechanical systems), even though it has a long history, established kingdoms, and old ruins. Magic covers combat, some daily conveniences (for those who can afford mages), and guild-controlled utilities — but common infrastructure for ordinary people is still primitive. That gap is Leandro's opening.

No electricity. All of Leandro's engineering work must be preindustrial: hydraulics, gears, pulleys, basic metallurgy, waterwheels, mechanical advantage — not steam/industrial revolution tech, and explicitly not weapons or anything that could "tip the balance" of the world's power structures (his self-imposed rule, ch04).

## Kingdom of Althea (home kingdom)

A border kingdom — sits directly against Demon Kin territory across the mountain range known to humans as **the Fangs**. This proximity is *why* Althea specifically performed the once-a-generation Hero summoning ritual: they're the frontline kingdom, the tripwire for the wider human territories.

### Cities
- **Selvane** — the capital. Site of the shrine of Everfall (where Leandro was summoned), the palace, and the crown. Politically central and comfortable — the seat of the false confidence General Vharek is counting on. *(Note: this is confirmed to eventually be destroyed by the Demon Kin invasion — see Notes/continuity.)*
- **Kaldreth** — a frontier city near the Fangs. Militarized, rougher, first line of real defense. Earmarked for an arc showing the war is worse near the border than the capital admits.
- **Bremmor** — mid-size interior trade city, agricultural surplus, calmer. Earmarked for one of Leandro's early "settle in and quietly fix things" arcs.
- **Ostwick** — a river/port city. Engineering-relevant (locks, mills, water trade) — earmarked as where his water magic + engineering brain gets to shine openly, before the shadow-work phase begins.

### Villages (smaller, visited via adventurer commissions)
- **Millbrook** — three days east of Selvane. Site of a newly uncovered ancient cave (found when a goat fell into a rockslide-sealed opening ~3 weeks before ch07). The village itself is in visible disrepair — bad roofs, a cracked/failing central well, half-tended fields — despite the nearby discovery that should have brought prosperity. Elder Petra was about to explain "bigger problems" than the cave when the current draft cuts off. **This cave is the planned unlock site for Leandro's Storage Box ability.**

## Neighboring Kingdoms (farther from the Demon Kin border, less immediately threatened — and, ironically, less prepared as a result)

- **Ravencourt** — wealthier, more magically advanced kingdom; home to a prestigious mage academy. Tentative home base for the (as-yet-unnamed) fire mage ally.
- **Dunmere** — smaller, insular mountain kingdom; more traditional/possibly isolationist toward outsiders. Earmarked for later political tension.
- **Thessary** — a coastal trade-federation rather than a single monarchy. Good fit for a merchant/logistics-driven arc later; plausible base for Cassian-style information networks.

## Demon Kin Territory
North of Althea, past the Fangs. Ruled by a war council that meets in a black-stone hall lit by mana-crystals. Patient, long-game strategists (see General Vharek Dromm in Characters) — they've spent years engineering bandit uprisings to thin Althea's border garrisons and have confirmed, via informants inside the shrine network, that the Hero summoning failed and the failure is being covered up by the crown.

## Geography notes
- Millbrook to Selvane: ~3 days travel by road.
- Exact relative distances/positions between Selvane, Kaldreth, Bremmor, Ostwick, and the neighboring kingdoms are not yet nailed down — treat current city order as narrative sequence, not confirmed cartography.
