# World Map

*Updated through ch89.*

Setting remains a classic sword-and-magic world that simply never developed engineering thinking, not a "lost technology" regression world. No electricity. Leandro's original rule against introducing advanced tech was explicit and self-imposed (ch04) — **the central arc of the whole story so far is that rule breaking down** under the pressure of war and grief (see plot/outline.md).

## Kingdom of Althea (home kingdom)
Border kingdom against Demon Kin territory across **the Fangs**.

### Cities / places
- **Selvane** — the capital. **Falls in ch16** — Demon Kin forces bypass the border garrisons and strike directly, the king and much of the inner court killed, outer districts burned for two days. Guildmaster Voss's "Last Watch" evacuation saves thousands of civilians at the cost of his own life and the Ninth Seat's. **As of ch58–60, Selvane's ruins are contested territory** — Demon Kin patrols, scavengers, and remnants of the crown's own recovery efforts all fighting over the wreckage, including the old palace vault (source of the "dragon stones" search). Threxis, the Eighth Seat, is killed here by Leandro's party in ch60.
- **Kaldreth, Bremmor** — established as frontier/interior cities in the original planning; not yet shown on-page in the chapters read.
- **Ostwick** — river/port merchant city, run more by a trade consortium than any lord. Leandro fixes the eastern lock gate's decade-old timing fault (ch14) with lock-keeper Yara. Base of operations for several months (ch14–23) before departing for Aldenreach.
- **Millbrook** — small village, ~3 days east of Selvane. Site of the ancient warded cave (ch07–11) that gives Leandro his storage ring and Cassian his soul-bound sword, at the cost of two guild surveyors' lives.

## Kingdom of Aldenreach (new — not in the original neighboring-kingdoms list)
- **Brindlemark** — chaotic, guild-heavy dungeon town built around an active, refreshing multi-floor dungeon that draws adventurers from multiple kingdoms. Leandro and Cassian relocate here (ch23–24) specifically to gain combat/team experience and "learn what's actually possible" beyond Althea's borders. They join Doran's established party and push from floor 16 to at least floor 51 (ch24–42).

## Kingdom of Ravencourt
- Wealthier, magically advanced, home to a prestigious mage academy — confirmed as originally planned. Far more heavily garrisoned and war-postured than Althea was (ch45).
- Home to **Seraphina ("the Ember Blade")**, who holds a defensive mountain pass three days west of the academy city, and — by ch54 onward — becomes the site of **Leandro's weapons workshop**, staffed with Borgrim (dwarf smith) and Mirelle (containment/refinement engineer).
- **The Island** — an uninhabited volcanic island a boat trip off Ravencourt's coast, used as a full-scale test site for Leandro's device (ch57). **Destroyed entirely** by the test — "not damaged, not cratered... gone." This event is the story's clearest confirmation that Leandro's weapon is a nuclear-weapon analog (explicit "chain reaction... not fission, but functionally, conceptually the same runaway principle" language in ch57).

## Aldervine
- A small, struggling farming town, formerly held generations back by minor noble **House Ashford**, whose lord was disgraced (grain-hoarding scandal during a lean year) and scattered. Strongly implied to be **Cassian's family and true origin** (ch85) — Leandro pieces this together but hasn't told him yet. Leandro scouts and later helps rebuild Aldervine (ch85–86); it becomes enough of a hub that Doran's party corresponds with the team regularly from there afterward (per ch87).

## Dunmere, Thessary
- As originally planned: Dunmere (insular mountain kingdom) and Thessary (coastal trade federation). Thessary now specifically confirmed as the registration point for the shell holding company behind the noble war-profiteering conspiracy (ch88) — a concrete plot use for a kingdom that was previously just a placeholder.

## Demon Kin Territory
North of Althea, past the Fangs. Ruled by **the Court of Twelve** (see bible/characters.md) from a black-stone hall lit by mana-crystals. A separate, unnamed **fortress** elsewhere (not the main Court hall) is where the Demon King is killed in ch65 — Leandro targeted what he believed was a lesser command chamber and got the Demon King by chance, since the Court's members were scattered across fronts at the time.

## Corren's Watch
A garrison post near where Doran's party's bodies are found (ch87), roughly two days' ride from the site itself, along "the eastern ridge road."

## Geography notes
- Millbrook to Selvane: ~3 days travel.
- Brindlemark to Ravencourt: ~2 weeks' travel (ch44 "The Long Road").
- Ravencourt's border to the academy city: multiple checkpoints, ~2 more travel days beyond the border (ch45).
- Seraphina's pass: ~3 days west of Ravencourt's academy city.
- Exact continental layout (how Aldenreach, Ravencourt, Dunmere, Thessary, and Althea relate directionally) still not mapped precisely — treat narrative sequence as the only confirmed ordering.
