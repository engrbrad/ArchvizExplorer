# Outline

## Premise
A nuclear engineer is unwillingly isekai'd — pulled by a botched Hero-summoning ritual that grabbed "the nearest available soul" instead of its intended champion. Not OP: weak, low-Novice magic. Comedy/adventure tone inspired by **Dr. Stone** (rebuilding practical, real-world engineering from scratch, one constrained step at a time) and **The Eminence in Shadow** (secretly doing the real work while maintaining a low-profile/underestimated public persona) — but reframed for a straight sword-and-magic world rather than a post-apocalypse: this world simply never developed engineering thinking, even though magic and swords are advanced. Structured **per-arc, Re:Zero style**: each arc is a self-contained location/problem with its own small cast, resolving with a partial reveal or new thread pulling toward the next arc, while a slow-burn mystery (his shadow reputation, why he was pulled instead of the intended Hero, the palace's eventual fall) runs underneath.

## Tone rules (don't drift from these)
- MC is genuinely **not OP** — no combat power, no smug superiority. His edge is caution, competence, and quiet observation, not force.
- Comedy comes from restraint and character (his refusal to exploit his own abilities, Cassian's shameless needling), never from making him look pathetic or making other characters cruel for no reason.
- No electricity, no advanced weapons, no "modern tech power fantasy" — his self-imposed rule against introducing anything that could tip the world's balance of power must hold. Engineering wins are small, plausible, and built from locally available materials.
- Pacing should be **slow and detailed**, especially early — real story-time spent settling into places and doing visible, unglamorous engineering work, before shadow work becomes necessary later.
- The people who first take him in (Rasmin, Yolanda) treat him decently despite the ritual's failure — deliberately subverting the "dumped/treated as trash" isekai trope.

## Skill-reveal order (locked)
1. **Phase** — has from the start (involuntary, defensive only, costs him a worsening headache per use)
2. **Water Manipulation** — learned gradually, early (ch02 onward)
3. **Earth Shaping** — learned gradually, mid-story (construction only, unusable in combat/danger, has a fatigue cost) — not yet introduced on-page
4. **Storage Box** — unlocked later, via the Millbrook ancient-cave arc — not yet unlocked

## Arc structure

### Arc 1 — Selvane / The Shrine (ch01–ch05, mostly complete)
Isekai incident → integration and training → declining the crown's offer to travel instead → registering as an adventurer → first real brush with death (three-horn bear) → discovering and choosing to hide the Phase ability. Establishes his personality, ethics, and starting skillset.

### Interlude — The Gathering Storm (ch06, complete)
Demon Kin POV. Establishes General Vharek Dromm's long-game strategy and the political landmine Leandro unknowingly avoided by leaving Selvane: the crown's cover-up of the ritual's failure, which the Demon Kin now intend to exploit by striking the capital directly.

### Arc 2 — Millbrook / The Ancient Cave (ch07, in progress)
Currently drafting. Leandro and Cassian take a guild survey commission to a newly uncovered ancient cave near the small village of Millbrook. The village itself is visibly struggling (bad roofs, failing well, half-tended fields) despite the nearby discovery — Elder Petra was about to reveal Millbrook's "bigger problems" when the draft paused.

**Intent for this arc (per author instruction):** slow down. Let Leandro spend real time doing open, uncredited-but-visible engineering fixes — wells, pulleys, irrigation, mill gearing — purely because he notices something broken and can't leave it alone, not as part of any grand plan. No secrecy needed yet at this stage; the shadow-work phase comes later. This is also the arc meant to unlock **Storage Box** via the cave itself.

### Future arcs (not yet drafted — sequencing intent only)
- One or more "settling in" arcs in other Althean cities (**Bremmor** — interior trade city, calm, good fit for open engineering work; **Ostwick** — river/port city, strong fit for water-magic-driven engineering; **Kaldreth** — frontier city near the Fangs, shows the war is worse near the border than the capital admits) — each city should teach him something new (materials, techniques, local problems) that feeds into his growing practical toolkit.
- Demon Kin attacks escalate and spread beyond background news — visible consequences (refugees, a city under threat) force Leandro to realize passive travel isn't enough.
- **Shadow work begins here** — not as a secret-identity power fantasy, but as the only responsible option for someone who still can't fight: he starts contributing anonymously/indirectly rather than putting a target on himself, echoing the same reasoning that made him hide the Phase ability.
- He travels beyond Althea and meets stronger, visible allies who can do what he can't (fight openly) while he supports logistically/structurally — starting with the **fire mage** (tentatively based in Ravencourt's mage academy), who was not in Althea when the capital fell.
- **Selvane's palace is destroyed** by the Demon Kin's invasion (per Vharek's plan from ch06) — delivered to the reader as background news reaching Leandro elsewhere, not a scene he's present for. Very few people ever knew he was the ritual's actual (failed) result, so he isn't connected to it publicly.
- Leandro grapples privately with guilt over the palace's fall, and the unanswerable question of whether a "real" Hero would have changed the outcome. This guilt becomes part of what drives his shift toward shadow work — a quiet response to a loss he couldn't have prevented and, at the time, had no way of knowing he'd need to.
