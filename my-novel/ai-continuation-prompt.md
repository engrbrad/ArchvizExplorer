## My inspirations

- **Dr. Stone** — the "rebuild civilization from first principles, with real constraints" energy. My main character isn't reinventing tech with a montage; every fix is grounded in materials he actually has access to.
- **The Eminence in Shadow** — quiet competence under a low profile, a character who does the real work without needing credit or attention.
- **Re:Zero** — the structural model: self-contained arcs, each with its own local cast and problem, but a slow-burn overarching mystery/dread running underneath the whole thing.

The tone started as comedy/adventure (a weak-magic, non-OP isekai protagonist whose "boring" real-world skills — engineering, sanitation, logistics — turn out to be quietly revolutionary in a world that never developed them). It has since drifted into much darker war-tragedy territory. I want your honest read on whether that drift is working, not just validation — see the "Tone drift" section of `plot/outline.md` for the specific tension I'm wrestling with.

## The core premise, in case you only skim

Leandro Valdez, a nuclear engineer, gets pulled from Earth by a botched Hero-summoning ritual in the border kingdom of Althea. He's not OP — weak water magic, an involuntary near-unkillable "Phase" ability that costs him a worsening headache each use, and a self-imposed rule against introducing any tech that could tip the world's balance of power. That rule is the whole story's central tension: over the course of the draft, grief and war pressure erode it completely, and by the current final chapter (89) he's built and used a nuclear-weapon-analog device, killed the Demon King, and is now lying to his own closest allies about the true scope of what he's building next after a found-family party of allies is murdered by a war-profiteering noble conspiracy.
