# Chapter 43 — The Road West

The road toward Ravencourt's territory — and whatever remained of Seraphina's western campaign — carried them through country neither of them had traveled, increasingly unsettled the further they went, small garrison towns visibly tenser than anything Leandro remembered from Althea's interior even at its worst.

The patrol found them on the fourth day, emerging from treeline cover with the particular, wrong-footed silence that meant they'd been tracking the road for a while before finally committing to an ambush — four Demon Kin scouts, lesser rank-and-file rather than anything approaching the Court's seated generals, but dangerous enough that Leandro felt the old, familiar spike of adrenaline the moment they broke cover.

"Contact!" Cassian's blade was already drawn, shifting to its longer form before the lead scout had fully closed distance.

Leandro fell into the same practiced role he'd built across fifty-one floors underground — water drawn from a nearby stream to blind and disorient, earth compacted beneath one scout's feet to disrupt its footing at a critical moment, careful, deliberate utility rather than direct confrontation. Cassian handled the actual killing blows, considerably more comfortable now with real combat than he'd been facing that three-horn bear a lifetime ago, his sword shifting fluidly between forms as the fight demanded.

It was close twice — a scout's claw raking close enough to Leandro's shoulder that only his own quick retreat saved him from a serious wound, and a moment where two scouts nearly flanked Cassian simultaneously before Leandro's improvised mud slick sent one sprawling at the crucial second. But they won, breathing hard, both bleeding from shallow wounds, standing over four fallen scouts in a quiet forest clearing.

"That's the first time either of us has actually fought Demon Kin directly," Cassian said, catching his breath, staring down at the fallen scouts with a complicated mix of relief and grim satisfaction.

"It won't be the last," Leandro said quietly, already tending to his own wound with practiced efficiency. "Not where we're headed."

They pressed on, wearier and more watchful than before, the encounter settling a new, sober weight over the remainder of their journey toward Ravencourt's territory and the mage whose reputation had first caught their attention months ago in a quiet harbor tavern.
