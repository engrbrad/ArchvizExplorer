# Chapter 34 — Recovery and the Choice

They spent a full day recovering in the nest's now-quiet chamber, Doran's sword pried carefully free from the broodmother's carapace once the venom-fatigue had passed enough for careful work. The blade had survived intact, though a hairline crack now ran along its lower edge — cosmetic damage more than functional, but a visible reminder of how close the fight had actually come.

"We could turn back here," Doran said, testing his ribs with careful movements, voice still rough from exhaustion. "Forty-six was the wall. We've broken it. Nobody would think less of this team for calling that success and heading home."

"Would you think less of it?" Leandro asked.

Doran considered the question seriously, the way he considered most things. "No," he admitted. "But I don't think any of us actually wants to stop here, either."

Nobody argued the point. Percival, still visibly shaken by what Cassian's blade had shown him was possible, seemed almost eager to continue if only to understand the sensation better. Bram simply nodded, characteristically brief. Ilyana voiced the group's shared sentiment plainly: "We came this far planning for the worst floor first. Let's at least see what forty-seven looks like before deciding we're done."

They pressed on the following morning, wounds bound, antidote reserves fully depleted and replaced instead with careful, renewed caution.
