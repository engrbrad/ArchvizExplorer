# Chapter 45 — Ravencourt's Edge

They reached Ravencourt's outer territory nearly two weeks after departing Brindlemark, the kingdom's border presenting a far more organized, heavily garrisoned face than anything they'd passed through in Althea — clear evidence of a kingdom taking the war considerably more seriously than complacency might have once allowed.

"This is more soldiers than I've seen in one place since Selvane," Cassian observed, watching a column move past their wagon with brisk, disciplined efficiency.

"Ravencourt's held its border better than most, according to the guild clerk," Leandro said. "This is probably why."

They were stopped twice more before reaching the academy city proper — checkpoints thorough but not hostile, soldiers clearly stretched thin but maintaining discipline despite it. It was at the second checkpoint, waiting for their papers to clear, that Leandro finally heard something concrete about their actual destination.

"The Ember Blade?" the checkpoint officer repeated, glancing up with mild surprise at the question. "She's not stationed here in the city, if that's what you're hoping. Word is she's holding a defensive line about three days further west — some pass the academy's been trying to reinforce for months now. If you're looking for her specifically, that's where you'll find her, assuming she hasn't been rotated somewhere else by the time you arrive."

Leandro exchanged a glance with Cassian, something settling into place — not quite anticipation, not quite dread, but the particular focused resolve of a long journey finally approaching its actual purpose.

"Three days," Cassian said, once they'd cleared the checkpoint and continued on. "You ready for whatever that's actually going to look like?"

"No," Leandro admitted honestly. "But I don't think readiness is really the point anymore. I just want to see what real, sustained conflict actually demands, up close, from someone who's been doing it for months without stopping."

Cassian nodded slowly, and they continued west, toward a defensive line and a mage whose reputation, however impressive from a distance, was about to become something considerably more immediate.
