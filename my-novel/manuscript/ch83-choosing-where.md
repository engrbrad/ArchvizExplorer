# Chapter 83 — Choosing Where

They spent the better part of a week debating where the reconstruction effort should actually go, the conversation circling through a dozen possibilities before finally settling on criteria rather than a single name.

"Not somewhere already thriving," Leandro said, laying out his own thinking. "Ravencourt's fine. The academy city doesn't need our help. I want somewhere that's been quietly struggling — not devastated enough to draw attention or aid from the crown or the academy, just... overlooked. The way Millbrook was, before I ever set foot there."

"Somewhere along the war's edge, then," Seraphina suggested. "Close enough to have felt the strain of refugees or supply shortages, far enough that it's never been a priority for anyone's official relief efforts."

Cassian, still carrying the faint, unresolved weight of their conversation from days earlier, offered a name almost carefully, like he was testing how it would land. "There's a town called Aldervine. Border region, southwest of here. Farming community, mostly — I passed through it once, years back. Didn't strike me as prosperous even then, and that was before two years of war strained everything downstream of it."

"You know it well?" Leandro asked, catching something in his friend's tone.

"Well enough," Cassian said, and didn't elaborate further, though the careful, deliberate way he didn't elaborate told its own quiet story.

Nobody pressed him on it. Leandro simply nodded, filing the detail away with the same patient care he'd given every other unanswered thread about his friend's past. "Aldervine, then. I'll go take a proper look before we commit any real resources — same as I'd do with any project. Assess first, act second."
