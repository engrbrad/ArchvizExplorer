# My Novel (untitled — nuclear engineer isekai)

Quick project overview for future-you (and me).

## Logline
A weak-magic, unwillingly-isekai'd nuclear engineer refuses a palace desk job to see a sword-and-magic world firsthand — armed with an involuntary near-unkillable Phase ability, gradually-learned Water and Earth magic, and a hard rule against introducing any tech that could tip the world's balance of power.

## Premise
Leandro Valdez is pulled from an ordinary night-shift life on Earth by a misfired Hero-summoning ritual in the border kingdom of Althea — the ritual grabbed the "nearest available soul" instead of the champion it was built for. Not OP, not treated as trash: the shrine that summoned him owns the mistake and treats him decently, and he responds by asking for an honest education about the world instead of taking the safety of a court position. Starts in the comedy/adventure spirit of *Dr. Stone* and *The Eminence in Shadow* — but the story has since grown into a much darker war narrative: Selvane's capital falls, a mentor dies holding the line, and Leandro ends up breaking his own founding rule against advanced weapons, building and using a nuclear-weapon-analog device that kills the Demon King. As of the latest chapter, he's grieving a murdered found-family party and quietly deceiving his own closest allies about the true scope of what he's building next. See `plot/outline.md` ("Tone drift") for a flagged decision point on whether this escalation is where the story is meant to go.

## Folder guide
- `manuscript/` — the actual chapters, in reading order (`ch01-anomaly.md`, `ch02-lessons.md`, ...)
- `bible/` — the reference material that keeps the story consistent
  - `characters.md` — names, arcs, relationships, voice notes
  - `world-map.md` — geography, locations, travel distances
  - `timeline.md` — in-story chronology
  - `glossary.md` — invented terms, factions, magic/tech rules
- `plot/` — the planning layer
  - `outline.md` — act/chapter beats
  - `notes.md` — loose ideas, continuity flags

## Workflow
1. Sketch/update beats in `plot/outline.md` before drafting a chapter.
2. Draft the chapter in `manuscript/`.
3. Update `bible/` with anything new the chapter introduces (a character trait, a place, a rule, a date).
4. Log any inconsistency you notice in `plot/notes.md` under "Continuity flags" so it doesn't get lost.
